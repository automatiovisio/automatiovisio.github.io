02Solutions

What types of processes can be automated?

Common examples include approvals, claims, purchase requests, stock management, fixed asset tracking, onboarding, surveys, document reviews, checklists, reporting and notifications.

How do I know if a process can be automated?

If a task is repetitive, follows a set of rules or needs the same information entered more than once, it is usually a strong candidate.

Will automation replace my team?

No. Automation removes repetitive admin so your team can spend their time on work that needs judgement and care.

Can an existing process be improved without replacing everything?

Yes. Many organisations see significant gains by improving the process they already have instead of starting over.

Can the system follow our own rules and approval levels?

Yes. Workflows, approval paths, limits and business rules are set up around how your organisation works.
