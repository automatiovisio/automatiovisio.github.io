FAQ

Straight answers.

What kind of businesses do you work with?

Any organisation with repetitive admin, approvals or manual tracking, including finance, operations, retail, F&B, logistics and service teams. If your team runs a process on paper, email chains or spreadsheets, we can help.

Do we need to buy new software?

Usually not. Wherever possible we build on platforms you already pay for, such as Microsoft 365, SharePoint and Power Automate. When a custom web app is the better fit, we explain why before we build it.

How long does a project take?

It depends on the process. Smaller workflows can often go live within weeks, and larger systems are delivered in stages so you see value early. You get a clear timeline before any work starts.

Is our data secure?

Your data stays in your own environment. We use role-based access so people only see what they should, and every action is logged in an audit trail.

Can we try StockSense?

Yes. Use the demo request on the home page and we will show you how StockSense would work for your stores.

What happens after launch?

We support your team through go-live, fix anything that comes up and help you improve or extend the system as your business changes.

Still have a question?

Ask us directly. We reply with a clear answer, not a sales pitch.
