01About AutomatioVisio

What does AutomatioVisio do?

We build websites, automate workflows and develop custom web-based systems. We also make our own product, StockSense, for counting stock on a phone.

Who is this for?

Any organisation that runs on repetitive admin, approvals or manual tracking, for example finance, operations, retail, F&B, logistics and service teams.

Do you provide ready-made products?

StockSense is our own product. Our websites, workflows and systems are built around your requirements instead of a generic template.

Why choose AutomatioVisio?

We focus on practical outcomes: simpler operations, better visibility and less manual effort, with clear communication in plain language throughout.

02Our services

Do you build websites?

Yes. Website development is one of our services: fast, mobile-friendly sites in your branding, with clear pages for your services and a way for customers to get in touch.

Can the website send enquiries to our email?

Yes. An enquiry form can email you automatically, and it can be connected to a workflow when you are ready.

What is the difference between workflow automation and system development?

Workflow automation moves work between people automatically, such as approvals and reminders. System development builds the place where the records, forms and reports live. Many projects use both.

What is StockSense?

StockSense is our own product for counting stock. Teams count on a phone, attach a photo as proof and submit, and management sees the result straight away.

What types of processes can be automated?

Common examples include approvals, claims, purchase requests, stock management, fixed asset tracking, onboarding, surveys, document reviews, checklists, reporting and notifications.

How do I know if a process can be automated?

If a task is repetitive, follows a set of rules or needs the same information entered more than once, it is usually a strong candidate.

Will automation replace my team?

No. Automation removes repetitive admin so your team can spend their time on work that needs judgement and care.

03Getting started

How does a project begin?

We start by understanding your current process, the problems it causes and the result you want. Then we propose a clear solution before anything is built.

What if I am not sure what I need?

That is perfectly fine. Most people start with a business problem, not a technical requirement. We help you identify the best approach.

How long does a project take?

It depends on the size and complexity of the work. Larger projects are delivered in stages so you see value early, and you get a clear timeline before work starts.

Can changes be requested while it is being built?

Yes. We build in stages so your team can test early and give feedback, and the solution is refined along the way.

Do you provide training?

Yes. We guide your team through go-live so people feel confident from day one.

04Security & data

Who owns the data?

Your organisation keeps ownership of its business data.

Can access be restricted?

Yes. Access can be set by role and responsibility, so people only see and do what they should.

Is activity tracked?

Yes. Audit trails can record submissions, approvals and changes, so you can see who did what and when.

Is our business information kept confidential?

Yes. Confidentiality is treated as a priority throughout every engagement.

05Mobile & access

Can the solution be used on mobile phones?

Yes. Our websites and systems are designed to work on desktops, tablets and phones.

Do users need to install anything?

In most cases, no. Solutions open directly in a web browser.

Can users upload photos and documents?

Yes. Many solutions support photo capture, attachments and supporting documents.

Can we try StockSense?

Yes. Use the demo request on the home page and we will show you how StockSense would work for your stores.

06Integration & growth

Can it work with the systems we already use?

Where it makes sense, yes. Solutions can be designed to work alongside your existing tools and workflows.

Can features be added later?

Yes. Solutions are built so new steps, forms, reports and users can be added over time.

What happens if our business grows?

You can start with one process and extend to more teams and workflows without starting from scratch.

07Pricing & support

How much does a project cost?

Cost depends on scope, features and complexity. After we understand your needs, you receive a clear quotation.

Are there recurring charges?

That depends on how the solution is set up and any third-party services involved. Every cost is discussed upfront.

Do you offer support after launch?

Yes. We support your team through go-live, fix anything that comes up and help you improve or extend the solution as your needs change.

Still have a question?

Ask us directly. We reply with a clear answer, not a sales pitch.
